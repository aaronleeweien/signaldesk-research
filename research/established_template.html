What we will actually do with this evidence

Investment decision: If an established ETF's live record is persuasive, buying the existing fund generally makes more sense than reconstructing its entire changing basket of stocks yourself. That is a DCA/investing choice, not a new daily entry signal.

Active trading research: We should only reconstruct a buy/sell strategy when the documented historical universe, exact membership changes, weights, rebalance timing and after-cost fills can be independently reproduced. SPMO/XMMO's live histories are good evidence for the underlying idea, but not proof that our homemade execution adds value over the fund.

Passive baseline: CSPX is still the Ireland-domiciled comparison for the user's investment implementation. It is not mixed into the daily US ETF sample because London hours and withholding differences require a separate after-tax analysis.
