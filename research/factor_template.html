What does the evidence say?

SPMO's rules systematically select stocks with high momentum scores from the S&P 500, reconstituting the fund twice yearly. The fund itself executes underlying constituent changes, so a retail investor need not trade 100 individual stocks.

SPMO sponsor expense ratio: 0.13% per year, already reflected in actual ETF NAV/price history. The simulation additionally assumes 0.1% per external ETF trade.

Exact trading discipline

Simple strategy: SPMO monthly accumulation. At each scheduled contribution, buy SPMO; otherwise hold. The ETF automatically replaces internal constituent stocks twice yearly.

Experimental relative-strength strategy: At the first US session each month, compare the completed prior six-month total return of SPMO and VOO. Hypothetically hold whichever ranked higher, switching at next month's opening if leadership changes.

Experimental defensive strategy: At the monthly review hold SPMO if it exceeds its prior 200-day moving average; otherwise use short Treasury ETF SHY.

These two overlays must demonstrate better risk-adjusted returns and meaningful incremental benefits against simply holding SPMO before being used for actual trades.
