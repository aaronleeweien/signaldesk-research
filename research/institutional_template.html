Investment thesis

Top-5 Momentum attempts to exploit persistence in medium-term stock performance. It ranks 30 established US stocks each month and hypothetically holds the five highest-ranked at 20% each. Academic literature supports momentum as a historical return pattern, but this specific implementation does not have unbiased proof of future alpha.

Risk-adjusted return decomposition

Monthly regression of the model against each benchmark, accounting for market exposure. Annual alpha is the estimated regression intercept, not guaranteed future profit.

Uncertainty & statistical strength

Paired three-month block bootstrap of monthly active returns (2,500 replicates), plus approximate Newey–West 3-lag inference. These statistics remain biased if the stock universe was selected with hindsight.

Entry and exit rulebook

ENTRY: At the next scheduled monthly rebalance, hypothetically buy the five eligible stocks with the highest fixed momentum score.

SIZE: Equal 20% target per stock; no margin, shorts or leverage.

EXIT: Sell a stock at the following monthly rebalance if it has fallen outside the top five.

NO INTRAMONTH STOP: This model has no validated intramonth stop-loss. A stock can fall sharply before the next exit date.
