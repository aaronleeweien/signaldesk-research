What is actually being researched?

01 / COMPARATORS

VOO DCA and QQQ DCA. Buy and hold, adding $100 every new month. QQQ is a higher-concentration comparison, not automatically a fair replacement for VOO.

02 / SECTOR LEADERSHIP

Sector Top 2, Core + Sector, and Sector Risk Weighted. Each calendar month rank nine established US sector ETFs using six-month and 12-minus-1-month returns; require a positive six-month return and price above its 200-day average. Defensive cash allocation is represented by SHY.

03 / RISK & PULLBACKS

VOO Volatility Target scales between VOO and SHY toward a 12% historical volatility target; VOO Pullback switches a 40% satellite between VOO and SHY after a short-term RSI(2) dip in an established uptrend.

DATA QUALITY

Yahoo adjusted daily prices are automatically downloaded; an incomplete US trading day is excluded. A missing or stale data feed stops updates, not silently fabricates signals.

TRANSACTION MODEL

Initial $2,000, additional $100 per calendar month, fractional ETF units, 10-bps per-side assumed execution cost, no borrowing, and simulated trades at next session's opening prices.

TESTING LIMITS

All historical periods have already happened; 2023 onward has already been studied. The selected model comes only from 2019–2022 and is then diagnosed over 2023 onward, not declared prospectively validated.
