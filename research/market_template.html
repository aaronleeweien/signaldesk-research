What this page does—and does NOT do

US exchange catalog: We collect tickers from Nasdaq and other US exchanges. They are possible assets to investigate, not securities whose valuations have all been measured.

Daily screening: We run several liquid-stock searches: large companies, strong recent price moves, fast earnings growth, and low price relative to earnings. We then request actual price histories for a smaller set. Counts above show what worked.

Valuation: Forward P/E means share price divided by expected next-year earnings. Earnings growth means how quickly profits are expected or reported to increase. These measures help judge a price, but cannot forecast a guaranteed future return. Missing numbers stay unknown.

Trading: Historical breakout profits on currently listed companies may look too good because past failures or delistings have disappeared from the sample. We will not label these setups proven.

Current coverage is US-listed securities only, not global stocks, bonds, futures, crypto or currencies. The screen does not individually reprice every catalogued security each day. No broker execution, no forecast price target, no guaranteed income.

SignalDesk uses third-party historical Yahoo market screens and public Nasdaq exchange directories. Market-data limits are shown rather than hidden. No live BUY recommendations.
