Exact rules the model actually tests

Daily scan: Each US market close, check tracked ETFs for three positive momentum conditions: price above its 200-day moving average, positive gains over the previous 63 and 126 trading sessions, and a close above the previous 20-session high.

Paper BUY: Add a newly qualifying breakout to the portfolio when there is room. Maximum two ETFs, equally weighted; invest unused slots in short-term Treasury ETF SHY.

Paper EXIT: Remove an ETF when its close falls below its previous 20-day low, 200-day average, or when its 63-day return is no longer positive. Hypothetical orders execute at the next US session's opening price.

Stock watchlist: The same breakout conditions are applied to our fixed 30-stock exploratory universe, but stock events are not portfolio trades because choosing surviving companies creates hindsight bias.

Notifications: Only brand-new events recorded before the following estimated US session opens are eligible for an assigned GitHub Issue. Monthly SPMO buying remains a separate strategy and is not changed by this scanner.
