Review the evidence gates

How decisions are made

Reject: The tested rule fails its predeclared performance, turnover, or risk tests. Do not act on its signals.

Paper-test: A rule survives retrospective screening. Record every subsequent prediction before the relevant market open and measure the actual following-session paper result.

Consider for live review: Meaningful prospective performance survives costs, benchmark comparisons and risk checks, with real execution feasibility separately verified. This would still require an explicit human decision, never automatic permission.

No automatic live-trading capability is connected to this dashboard.

Our objective forward-validation protocol

We will record

Timestamped ETF target weights, new market-session opens and closes, simulated fills with explicit costs, daily account balance, and matched VOO/SPMO passive benchmarks. New data must arrive after the original prediction is recorded.

Minimum evidence window

63 genuinely future US trading sessions across at least three calendar months, plus a review of drawdowns, turnover and benchmark-relative returns. That's a preliminary screen, not guaranteed statistical proof of profitability.

How this prevents misleading results

We will not silently change the rules that generated earlier predictions, backdate paper trades, or declare a strategy superior because it won a few trades. Every future alteration gets a new protocol version and its own prospective evaluation period.

Keep investing in your chosen long-term ETF separately. Failed active models should not divert that capital.
